THE ROUND

  • Runners wait 1:30 in the lobby while the chasers set traps. Then the barrier drops.
  • There are always at least 3 chasers. Nobody guards the prison, but an alarm tells the chasers the moment someone starts untying a prisoner.

HOW RUNNERS WIN

  • Rising tide: the first 4½ minutes are dry. Then the sea comes in, and the dry land shrinks towards the prison (blue on the map). Everyone wades slower in the flood. When the sea reaches an oil leak the can floats loose at the waterline. At 0:00 only the raised prison and the crates and pillars around it are above water.
  • The ship can be lost: once half the island is under water (about 2½ minutes before the end, shown under the timer) the storm sweeps the ship away. After that only the lifeboat is left.
  • Lifeboat: an orange lifeboat stands beside the prison. When the island is flooded at 0:00 it floats off with every runner who is still free, so the runners win if even one of them was never caught or was rescued in time. This is the only way out once the fuel is gone.
  • Storm: from half time it rains. Running is a little slower and anyone who runs outdoors, runner or chaser, can slip and fall. Walking, crouching and being indoors are safe.
  • Rebuild the ship: the ship at the dock is a wreck. It needs 20 planks (driftwood on the beaches, brown dots on the map; you carry up to 4), 3 iron parts (red ring, inside buildings; the chasers want the same parts for their chain) and 3 fuel cans. Walk onto the dock to hand everything in; the sign above the ship shows what is still missing. Each plank and iron part slows you a little, and you drop them if you are beaten down.
  • Fuel: every runner carries a shovel. Go to one of the eight oil leaks (orange dots on the map; a red ✕ means you saw it was burned, a grey ring means it was already dug up) and stand still on it for 8 seconds to dig up a can. Digging is loud and the nearest chaser hears it. The can is heavy: you run slower and show on the chasers' map until you reach the dock on the east shore. When the planks, the iron and the three cans are all in, a 25-second countdown starts. Walk onto the ship to sit down; it leaves once 60% of the runners are seated. Chasers can board and shove seated runners overboard.
  • Dock blockade: chasers can search the buildings for iron parts (red ring). Three parts forge a heavy chain; the chaser carries it to the bollard at the far end of the dock and stands there 3 seconds to lock the ship. A chained ship cannot leave, even when it is fuelled and full. Runners must find an axe (blue ring, inside buildings), stand still by the bollard and chop for 6 seconds. Chopping is loud and calls the chasers. There are nine iron parts, so the chasers can chain the ship up to three times.

HOW CHASERS WIN

  • Capture every runner before the island floods. Burning fuel and chaining the ship stop the early escape and keep the runners on the shrinking island with you.
  • Burn fuel: stand still on an oil leak for 4 seconds, or walk over a dropped can.

CAPTURE AND RESCUE

  • A runner at zero health is beaten down, carried to the prison (grey square on the map) and tied to a post.
  • After 30 seconds they recover. A teammate who stands beside them for a few seconds unties them.

HIDING

  • You can hide for 10 seconds, then you cough and give yourself away.
  • If a chaser sees you climb in, he comes and drags you out.
  • Chasers only see you with a clear line of sight, so standing behind a wall or crate hides you even up close. They do hear running footsteps nearby; crouching and walking are silent.
  • Touch a chaser from behind before he notices you and he freezes for 5 seconds.
  • Long stone walls have low gaps: yellow ones need a crouch, orange ones need you prone. Bots cannot use them.

ITEMS AND TRAPS

  • Runners: vest, boost shoes, smoke bomb, decoy, banana peel, medkit. Grab the chasers' nets and throw them in the ocean. Jiggle left and right when a chaser aims his hook and it misses.
  • Chasers: the hook is built in (7 s cooldown); nets and boost shoes are picked up; pit, water, hanging and bear traps are set.
  • Traps hold: a runner caught in a pit, snare or bear trap cannot get out alone. A teammate must stand next to them and press G (push); this works even while the push is on cooldown. After 40 seconds they wriggle free on their own. Runner push cooldown is 10 seconds, chaser 5.
  • Thunder mountain (⛰ on the map): a tall mountain on the shore. A spiral path winds three times round it to the top and monsters guard it. Push a monster (G) to shove it off the mountain for 12 seconds, or sprint past before it smashes you. On the summit is one lightning orb; press X to strike every chaser and freeze them for 3 seconds. One use per round.
  • Decoy: the copy runs away from chasers like a real runner for 16 seconds and most chasers follow it. Jiggling left and right in front of a sprinting chaser often makes him slip.
  • Ocean crocodiles: crocodiles patrol the sea all round the island. Swim out to escape a chaser and they come for you and bite. Boats, the dock and the ship are safe.
  • Crocodiles: when half the island is flooded the ship sails off and crocodiles come in with the water. They swim after any runner wading nearby and bite for a little damage; anything dry is safe.
  • Trap kits lie around the island. A runner who finds one gets a random trap to set for the chasers; a chaser who grabs it gets one extra trap.
  • Around the island: ziplines from rooftops, trampolines, ladders, rowing boats on the rivers.
